Profit finder

Scans every item in a category and ranks them by profit after your return rate, tax and fees. Materials are bought and the item is sold in the city you picked at the top. For the Black Market, materials are priced in Caerleon.

Pick a category and press Scan. A scan asks the price server for every item and material, so big categories take a few seconds.
